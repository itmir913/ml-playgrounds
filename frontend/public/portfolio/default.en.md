## Topic and problem statement {#topic}

Write what problem you set out to solve or explore with machine learning, and say whether it is a classification, regression, or clustering task.

## Why this topic {#motivation}

Write why you picked this topic and what drew you to the field. If it connects to a career you are interested in, write how.

## Choosing and understanding the data {#data}

Write which data you used: where it came from, how much of it there is (rows and columns, or number of images), what the main variables are, and why you chose it. If the data carries any bias or raises an ethical concern, write that too.

## Preparing the data and choosing features {#preprocessing}

Write what you did about blanks and values outside the normal range, which features you trained on and why you picked them, and how you split the data into training and test sets.

## Choosing a model and running experiments {#model}

Write which models you used and why you thought they suited this problem. If you compared models or changed their settings, write what differed and which one you kept.

## Results and error analysis {#result}

Write how the model scored and on which metrics. Look at what it got right and what it got wrong, and write where the errors happened and what you think caused them.

## Limits and what to improve {#limitations}

Write what the limits of your data and your model were. Write what you would improve to get better results, or to use this on a real problem.

## Solving problems, and what I learned {#reflection}

Write about the hardest problem you ran into and how you worked through it. Write what you learned, how you grew, and what you would do differently next time.
